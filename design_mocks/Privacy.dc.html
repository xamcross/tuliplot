Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 July 2026

This policy explains what TulipLot collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we store

Your account email, an encrypted password (or Google account identifier), and your dashboard layout — the URLs and titles of the cells you configure. That's what makes your grid follow you between devices.

Embedded sites

TulipLot loads the sites you add inside sandboxed frames. We don't read, store, or proxy the contents of those frames — they talk to their own servers directly, just as they would in a browser tab.

Advertising

Free accounts show a single ad in the sixth cell. Premium accounts serve no ads and set no advertising cookies. Upgrading removes ad-related tracking entirely.

Billing

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never receive or store your card details — only a customer reference used to manage your subscription.

Your rights

You can export or delete your dashboard and account at any time. For data requests, email privacy@tuliplot.app and we'll respond within two business days.
