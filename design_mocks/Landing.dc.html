Questions

Can I embed any website?

Any HTTPS URL. Some sites block embedding — the optional Chrome companion unlocks most of them.

Is my data private?

Your dashboard is tied to your login and synced only to your account. Frames are sandboxed.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

You drop back to the free 5-cell layout; the 6th app is parked so you can re-place or discard it.
