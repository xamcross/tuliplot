# Five ways to lay out a productive dashboard

Six cells is enough for a focused command center — if you arrange them with
intent. Here are patterns that work.

## 1. Put your inbox top-left

We read screens top-left first. Give that spot to whatever you check most —
usually email or chat.

## 2. Group by rhythm, not by app type

Cluster the things you glance at constantly (calendar, tasks) apart from the
things you dip into occasionally (docs, analytics).

## 3. Keep one “focus” cell

Reserve a cell for whatever you are actively working in today, and swap it as
your focus changes.

## 4. Use the companion sparingly

Only unlock the sites you truly need composited. Fewer heavy frames means a
snappier grid.

## 5. Let quiet cells sleep

DashDash unmounts cells you are not looking at to save memory. Put rarely-used
apps where they can rest.

Small layout choices compound. Spend five minutes arranging, save an hour of
friction.
