# Getting started with TulipLot

TulipLot gives you a fixed **3×2 grid** — six cells, each hosting a live web
app. This guide walks you from sign-up to a working dashboard.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up with email and a password, or use **Sign in with Google**. Your
dashboard is tied to your account and syncs wherever you log in.

## 2. Add your first app

Click any empty cell and choose **Add a site**. Pick from the curated catalog
(Gmail, Google Calendar, Trello, and more) or paste any HTTPS URL. TulipLot
validates the URL and loads it inside a sandboxed frame.

## 3. Arrange the grid

Drag any cell onto another to **swap** their positions. The layout is fixed at
six cells, so you always know where each app lives.

## 4. Free vs Premium

On the free plan, the bottom-right cell shows a single ad and you get five
usable cells. Upgrading to Premium removes the ad and unlocks all six.

You are ready to go. The next guide covers adding sites that normally refuse to
be embedded.
